The Problem

Communities face disasters without the right tools.

In Mexico, natural disasters displace thousands yearly. Existing alert systems send generic notifications — but don't tell people what to do, where to go, or how to stay connected when networks fail.


The gap between an alert and survival is actionable, real-time, adaptive guidance. That's exactly what ResQ+ delivers.

What We Built

Not just an app — a survival ecosystem.

Every feature was designed around one question: what do people actually need in the worst moment of their lives?

🧠
AI Disaster Detection
Identifies the emergency type via geolocation and sensor data, delivering specific step-by-step guidance for earthquakes, floods, fires, and more.
🗺️
Dynamic Evacuation Routes
Real-time routing that adapts as users report blocked roads. No static maps — the path evolves with what's happening on the ground.
📡
Offline Mesh Network
When cell towers go dark, ResQ+ switches to Bluetooth mesh — keeping communities connected without any internet access.
🎙️
Voice Activation
Trigger emergency protocols hands-free. Say "HELP," "FIRE," or "QUAKE" and the app auto-activates GPS, calls, and audio recording.
🫀
Immobility Detection
If no movement is detected, ResQ+ assumes the user may be trapped — auto-broadcasting their location with a distress sound signal.
📊
Government Dashboard
Real-time aggregated data for emergency coordinators — hazard maps, active SOS signals, and road status for faster decisions.

Process

From alert to safety in four steps.

ResQ+ closes the loop between detection, communication, action, and community recovery.

01
Detect & Classify
AI analyzes incoming signals and classifies the disaster type instantly, triggering the right protocol for that specific emergency.
02
Alert & Guide
Users receive clear, actionable instructions — not vague alerts. Voice, visual, and haptic guidance activates simultaneously.
03
Connect & Navigate
Mesh network activates. Community members share live hazard reports. Dynamic routes update in real time.
04
Recover & Learn
Incident data feeds the government dashboard and improves AI models. Communities grow more prepared with every event.
