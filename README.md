## About
This landing page communicates the ResQ+ vision: a community-oriented resilience ecosystem intended to support people before, during and after disaster scenarios. It is the storytelling companion to the interactive [ResQ+ Prototype](https://github.com/abrilvillita/ResQ-Prototype).

## Highlights
- Editorial hero and problem/solution narrative
- Four-step emergency journey
- Conceptual impact metrics
- English and Spanish toggle
- Animated counters
- Responsive dark visual system
- Social call to action

## Technology
HTML5 · CSS3 · Vanilla JavaScript · Responsive design · Static deployment-ready structure

## Status
**Concept and competition proposal.** Claims and metrics represent the proposed product vision; they are not evidence of a deployed public-safety service.

## Product direction explored
Adaptive instructions · Dynamic routes · Public-sector dashboards · Community participation · Resilient communication · Voice-assisted interaction
